Sa-relative swaras · flute-specific holes

The six dots in each row are holes 1–6, from nearest the blowing end to furthest away. Swara identity comes from the selected reference bansuri’s Sa and the chosen thaat. The Western pitch is a separate sounding-pitch readout; it does not rename the swara.

Reference Sa vs. mapped flute

The reference bansuri defines the real-world pitch of Sa. For example, choosing an E reference bansuri means H1–H3 closed is called Sa and sounds E. If the flute being mapped is C, the app finds the C-flute fingering that reproduces that same E pitch, while keeping the swara label as Sa.

Practical tuning note

Six-hole fingering charts are reference patterns. Half-hole size, cross-fingerings, breath pressure and octave response vary between bamboo flutes and teachers. Tune each note against your chosen Sa drone; use the diagram as a starting point.
